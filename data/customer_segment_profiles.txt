CUSTOMER SEGMENT PROFILES

Champions:
- High tenure
- High revenue contribution
- Strong engagement
- Low churn probability

Loyal Customers:
- Consistent engagement
- Long-term relationship
- Moderate revenue contribution

New Customers:
- Recently acquired
- Low tenure
- High onboarding potential

Price Sensitive:
- Cost conscious
- Highly responsive to discounts
- Moderate churn risk

At Risk:
- High churn probability
- Declining engagement
- Requires proactive retention
