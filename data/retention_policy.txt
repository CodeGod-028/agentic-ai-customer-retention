CUSTOMER RETENTION POLICY

Objective:
Reduce customer churn and improve customer lifetime value through targeted retention initiatives.

Retention Priorities:

1. High Risk Customers
- Immediate intervention required
- Escalation within 24 hours
- Personalized retention offers

2. Medium Risk Customers
- Proactive engagement
- Value-added offers
- Customer education campaigns

3. Low Risk Customers
- Loyalty maintenance
- Engagement nurturing
- Premium service promotion

Approved Retention Offers:

- 10% Cashback
- 15% Discount Upgrade
- 20% Cashback
- Reward Point Bonus
- Premium Loyalty Reward
- VIP Benefits Package

Preferred Contact Channels:

High Value Customers:
- Phone Call
- Relationship Manager

General Customers:
- Email
- SMS
- In-App Notifications

Expected Retention Uplift:

High Priority Campaigns:
15%-25%

Medium Priority Campaigns:
10%-15%

Low Priority Campaigns:
5%-10%
