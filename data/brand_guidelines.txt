BRAND COMMUNICATION GUIDELINES

Tone:
Professional

Style:
Customer-Centric

Do:
- Be helpful
- Be concise
- Focus on value
- Focus on benefits

Do Not:
- Use aggressive sales language
- Use threatening retention language
- Overpromise outcomes

Campaign Structure:

1. Greeting

2. Customer Appreciation

3. Offer Presentation

4. Business Benefit

5. Call To Action

6. Professional Closing
